Why macro-F1, not accuracy: ChatGPT makes up over half the sample, so a model that always guessed "ChatGPT" would score well on raw accuracy while ignoring Claude and Gemini entirely. Macro-F1 weighs all three classes equally, which is why the winning model is chosen by that score rather than accuracy alone.
